About Peet

Local knowledge, hands-on building.

Peet Neethling has been building and consulting on homes in Klein Brak River and the surrounding coastal towns for years, working directly with homeowners from the first site visit through to the final coat of paint.

Every job starts with a walk of the site — understanding the ground, the wind, the neighbours, and what the client actually needs — before a single quote goes out.
