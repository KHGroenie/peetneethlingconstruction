About Peet

Honouring the past, building the future.

Peet Neethling Construction (Pty) Ltd works across two disciplines most builders don't combine: the careful restoration of historical buildings, and the design and construction of modern custom homes.

Each historical project is approached with respect for original stonework, timber and detail that gave the building its character in the first place.

On new builds, that same attention to detail carries over. Every job starts with understanding the site, the ground, the wind, the surroundings and what the client actually needs before a quote goes out.

Based in Mossel Bay, the team takes on projects across the Garden Route and Cape Winelands.
